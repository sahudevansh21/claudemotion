# AegisLaunch — 30 s launch film (1920×1080, 30 fps)

`src/compositions/Aegis/` · timing `aegis.json` · audio `scripts/aegis/generate_audio.py`
· site captures `scripts/aegis/capture_site.mjs`.

**Reference grammar** (supplied Nyx feature video, 24.5 s, music only): bold
title cards with a small mono label → zoomed screen-recording clips of the real
site → title → clips → logo end card. Titles de-blur in; one line swaps in place
("Ask anything / about Nightshift / about the world" → "Every trade, / small
ones run. / big ones wait. / rogue ones stop.").

**Theme** (aegisnet.solrun.fun): pure black, white Inter headlines with one
italic serif word, mono chips, silver particle wave (rebuilt procedurally after
the site hero), the two-bar mark redrawn as vector.
