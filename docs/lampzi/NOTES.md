## Sound
Only the supplied "SaaS SFX Pack [@MahdiEdits_]": Bleep Bloop at loader starts, Click on swaps and rows, the three whooshes on camera moves, Riser into the dialog press and the brand reveal, the bright hit on the dark switch and logo, and a 2 s slice of Gears under the wall of work. Mixed to -14 LUFS, peak below -1 dBFS. No other music.
