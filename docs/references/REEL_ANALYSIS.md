## Type treatment (the signature)

- **Text is blended into the picture, not laid on top.** Letters take on the colour
  and brightness of what is behind them (a blend mode like overlay / soft-light at
  partial opacity): they wash out over bright areas and almost vanish over
  highlights, so the line feels printed onto the footage.
- Slight **RGB split / chromatic fringe** on the letters (red/cyan edges), adding to the
  "lens" feel.
- Hook line: red (#e01a1a-ish), heavy grotesk, tight negative tracking, lowercase,
  ends with a period, about 1/3 frame wide, centred at ~45% height.
- Montage line: white, same font, much smaller (~1/4 frame wide), lowercase with a
  period, locked in place at frame centre for the whole montage. It never animates;
  only the footage changes underneath it.
- No captions or subtitles were added in the edit (the yellow italic line at ~9.5 s
  belongs to the source clip on a monitor).
