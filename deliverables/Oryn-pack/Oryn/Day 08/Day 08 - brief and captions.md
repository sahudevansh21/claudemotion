## A2 · Article (short) · How to read a frozen price
Format: ~200 words
Objective: Explain Market closed vs Stale, a common mistake.

**Outline**
- Hook: Two things look identical on a chart: a flat line because the market is closed, and a flat line because the chain stopped.
- Market closed: Underlying exchange is closed; oracle price is frozen at the last close, as expected. oNVDA and oQQQ on Ethereum opens in 6h.
- Stale: Arbitrum head is not advancing. Not a market closure; do not treat as a weekend freeze. (dMSFT on Arbitrum.)
- Close: One verdict, two very different next steps.

**Caption**
Flat price? Check why. Market closed and Stale look the same on a chart and mean opposite things.
