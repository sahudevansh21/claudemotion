## A4 · Article (short) · A bond is not an asset
Format: ~220 words
Objective: Share Oryn’s view on trust: proofs vs existence.

**Outline**
- Hook: A bond proves skin in the game, not that an offchain asset exists.
- Body: Tokenised assets promise exposure to something offchain. Collateral posted by an issuer shows commitment; it does not show the underlying exists or that onchain state matches it.
- What Oryn does: compare onchain state to issuer state and say so: Open (matches), Divergent (does not), or Unknown (not actionable).
- Close: Unknown means not actionable.

**Caption**
A bond proves skin in the game, not that an offchain asset exists. Here’s how we think about verifying tokenised assets.
