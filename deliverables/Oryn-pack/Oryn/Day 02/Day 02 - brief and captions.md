## P2 · Post (carousel, 7 slides) · The six verdicts
Format: 4:5, 1080x1350 design canvas, rendered at 2x (2160x2700) as JPEG q94 per slide
Objective: Teach the verdict vocabulary so every later post makes sense.

**On-image copy**
- Slide 1 cover: “Six verdicts. One value.”
- Slide 2 Open (green): Redemptions open, NAV fresh, onchain state matches issuer.
- Slide 3 Market closed (cream/blue): Underlying exchange is closed; oracle price is frozen at the last close, as expected.
- Slide 4 Action pending (yellow): event not yet applied onchain, e.g. xAAPL dividend $0.27 / share.
- Slide 5 Redemption closed (red): Fund redemption window is shut; collateral cannot be redeemed at published NAV.
- Slide 6 Stale (lavender): Arbitrum head is not advancing. Not a market closure.
- Slide 7 Divergent (black/red): issuer event not applied onchain, e.g. Stock split 3:1 not applied on Solana.

**Caption**
Six verdicts. Open, Market closed, Action pending, Redemption closed, Stale, Divergent. Swipe for what each one means and why “market closed” is not the same as “broken”.
