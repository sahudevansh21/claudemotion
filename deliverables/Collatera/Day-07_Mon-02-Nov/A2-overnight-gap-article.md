# The overnight gap: when stock markets close and DeFi loans don't

*Collatera · Article 02 · Day 7*

Crypto never sleeps. Stock exchanges do. That mismatch is the whole story of the overnight gap.

When a tokenized stock is used as collateral, the loan lives on-chain around the clock, but the asset it represents has a home exchange with opening hours. Between the close and the next open, news still happens: earnings, guidance, macro data. The on-chain price can drift, freeze or depend on thinner liquidity.

When the exchange reopens, the real price can jump straight past levels your position was relying on. That jump is the gap, and a buffer that looked comfortable at the close might not be at the open.

Three questions are worth asking about any tokenized-collateral position:

1. Is the underlying market open right now?
2. How old is the oracle's last update?
3. How much buffer would a gap of 5, 10 or 20 percent use up? *(hypothetical scenarios, not forecasts)*
